Jae-Yoon Kim

I am an assistant professor of economics at Pusan National University. I work on macroeconomics and monetary policy — how supply chain pressures pass through to inflation, and what automation and an ageing population do to pension systems and to the conduct of policy. Most of my empirical work is Bayesian and time-varying.
